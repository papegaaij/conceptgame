# The belt

## Summary

The asteroid belt: drifting rock fields and hundreds of hollowed-out mining stations, many
chartered to Helix Dynamics. Setting for Act 5, where the Ascendancy fights openly alongside
the Vrell and the stations must choose sides. Ends with the Iron Sovereign battle station.

## Design

### Look & feel

Frontier and industrial. Tumbling asteroids, some carved into stations with lit windows and
docking arms; ore haulers, conveyor tubes, refineries glowing orange. Split loyalties show in
the architecture: Coalition grey-white stations next to sleek black-and-gold Helix facilities.
Touchstones: *Outland* (1981), the belt in *The Expanse*, Descent's mines.

### Sub-locations

| Sub-location | Description |
|---|---|
| Open rock field | Drifting asteroids of all sizes; ambushes from behind rocks. |
| Ceres Hub | The biggest belt station: a hollowed dwarf planet with docks. |
| Mining station interiors | Flying through the tunnels of a hollowed asteroid (narrow, walls). |
| Refinery | Molten ore, furnaces, conveyor lines; heat and fire hazards. |
| Helix yards | Ascendancy shipyards hidden in the rocks; the Iron Sovereign. |

### Parallax layers

| Layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| deep | Distant asteroids, the Sun as a bright point, faint dust band. |
| ground | Large asteroid surfaces and station hulls below the player: turrets, docks, mines. |
| low-air | Smaller rocks drifting below, haulers, conveyor tubes. |
| air | Player's plane; drifting asteroids that block and can be destroyed. |
| high-air | Close rock fragments and dust passing over the player. |

### Palette & lighting

- Dark brown-grey rock, harsh distant sunlight from top-left, very high contrast.
- Orange refinery light; cool white station lights.
- Ascendancy structures gloss black with gold highlights and red lights — new to the palette.

### Hazards & set pieces

- **Drifting asteroids**: on the air layer, rocks drift across the field; small ones break
  into fragments when shot, big ones must be avoided. Enemies hide behind them.
- **Tunnels**: inside stations the play field narrows with walls, branches and blast doors.
- **Mining charges**: explosive charges on rocks; shooting one blasts everything nearby
  (usable against enemies).
- **Mass relay flash**: Ascendancy rail cannons fire across the whole screen after a red
  telegraph line. (Signature set piece: station choosing sides — a station's lights switch
  from white to red mid-level and its turrets turn on the player.)

### Natives

- Ascendancy: precision fighters, shielded gunships, cloaked ambushers, rail turrets.
- Vrell: swarms working with the Ascendancy, rock-borers that hide in asteroids.
- Coalition: loyal stations, miners' militia (allied on background layers).

### Music mood

Industrial and aggressive. Metallic percussion, distorted bass, the Ascendancy leitmotif
(brass, stately and cold) introduced here.
