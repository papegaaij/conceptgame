# Jovian system

## Summary

Jupiter: an endless sky of storm bands with gilded cloud cities floating in it, and Callisto,
where the Ascendancy has its fortress HQ. Setting for Act 6, the hunt for Vorne, ending with
his flagship, the Ascendant.

## Design

### Look & feel

Opulent and violent. Towering cloud banks in cream, ochre and rust; lightning; the Great Red
Spot on the horizon. Cloud cities are gilded domes and spires hanging from huge balloons and
anti-grav rings. Callisto is dark, cratered ice with a black fortress city carved into it.
Mid-act, the Vrell turn on the Ascendancy: the gold cities burn. Touchstones: Bespin in *The
Empire Strikes Back*, Art Deco, Einhänder's opulent stages.

### Sub-locations

| Sub-location | Description |
|---|---|
| Upper atmosphere | Open storm bands, lightning, helium-3 harvesters skimming the clouds. |
| Aurelia | The first and grandest cloud city; site of the 2166 riots. |
| The Great Red Spot | A storm eye with violent winds and electric discharges. |
| Io refineries | Volcanic moon: Ascendancy fuel and sulphur refineries, eruptions that throw debris into the sky. |
| Callisto approach | Orbital defences, Ascendancy fleet, the ice moon below. |
| Callisto HQ | The black citadel: hangars, rail cannons, Vorne's flagship docked. |

### Parallax layers

| Layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| deep | Deeper cloud bands scrolling slowly, lightning deep below, Jupiter's curve at high altitude. |
| ground | Cloud-city decks and domes, harvesters, turrets (for Callisto: the ice surface and fortress). |
| low-air | Balloons, anti-grav rings, cables, lower cloud wisps. |
| air | Player's plane. |
| high-air | Thick clouds and storm fronts passing over the player; lightning flashes. |

### Palette & lighting

- Cream, ochre, rust and brown storm bands; sunlight diffused and warm.
- Cloud cities gold and white with black accents; red Ascendancy lights.
- Lightning flashes briefly light the whole scene blue-white.
- Callisto: dark blue-grey ice, the black citadel, red and gold lights.
- Burning cities (after the Vrell turn): fire orange and Vrell teal together.

### Hazards & set pieces

- **Wind shear**: bands of wind that push the player and slow or speed up bullets.
- **Lightning strikes**: telegraphed columns of lightning that damage everything on the air
  layer, enemies included.
- **Falling city**: a cloud city loses its balloons and sinks through the layers as the player
  fights above and then alongside it. (Signature set piece.)
- **Three-way battle**: Vrell and Ascendancy fight each other and the player at once.

### Natives

- Ascendancy: full fleet — fighters, gunships, rail platforms, cloaked units, Vrell hybrids.
- Vrell: storm-riding swarms, gas-bag organisms; turning on the Ascendancy mid-act.
- Coalition: the assault fleet led by Okafor (allied, background layers).

### Music mood

Grand and furious. The Ascendancy leitmotif at full orchestra-synth strength; the main theme
fights back. Most bombastic act.
