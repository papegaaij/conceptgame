# Earth orbit

## Summary

Where the war begins. The Gagarin shipyards, orbital defence platforms and a thousand
satellites hang above a bright blue Earth — and the first Vrell swarms tear through them.
Setting for the first levels of Act 1.

## Design

### Look & feel

Hopeful and then broken. A clean, busy near-Earth space full of human engineering: lattice
shipyards, rotating habitat rings, solar arrays, cargo tugs. As the act progresses the same
structures burn, drift and break apart. Touchstones: Wing Commander hangar shots, the opening
of *Independence War*, Raptor's clean metallic sprites.

### Sub-locations

| Sub-location | Description |
|---|---|
| Gagarin shipyards | Lattice docks with half-built frigates, cranes, welding sparks. |
| Orbital defence ring | Chain of gun platforms and sensor stations; some already overrun. |
| Debris field | Wrecks of the first battle, spinning slowly; later levels. |
| Space elevator top | The Quito elevator anchor station, the tether running down to Earth. |

### Parallax layers

| Layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| deep | Earth's curve with clouds and night-side city lights; the Moon; starfield. Very slow. |
| ground | Large station hulls and shipyard decks the player flies "over": hull plating, hangar doors, turrets mounted on the hull. |
| low-air | Lattice beams, antennae and cranes passing under the player; low drones. |
| air | Player's plane: fighters, swarms, bullets. |
| high-air | Close drifting debris, solar-panel fragments, cloud of sparks passing over the player. |

In space, "ground" means large structures below the player's plane.

### Palette & lighting

- Hard, white sunlight from top-left; deep black shadows (no atmosphere).
- Earth adds a blue bounce light from below on large structures.
- Hulls in Coalition grey-white with navy and orange; warning lights blink red.
- Keep backgrounds low in teal/violet so Vrell bullets pop.

### Hazards & set pieces

- **Drifting debris**: slow-moving wreck chunks on the air layer that block bullets (yours and
  theirs) and damage on contact.
- **Shipyard crane**: a moving crane arm sweeps across the play field; fly under it at the
  right time. (Signature set piece.)
- **Evacuation shuttles**: friendly shuttles cross the screen; protecting them is an objective.
- **Station breaking up**: a habitat ring cracks and a section drifts across the background.

### Natives

- Vrell: light swarm fighters, spore carriers, boarding pods that latch onto stations.
- Coalition: friendly defence platforms (can be overrun and turned into hazards), shuttles.

### Music mood

Urgent, heroic, hopeful. Driving tempo, bright lead melody — the "main theme" act. The theme
returns in darker versions later in the campaign.
