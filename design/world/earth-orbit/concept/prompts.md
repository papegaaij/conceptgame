# Earth orbit – generator notes and prompts

## debris-final-r16-a

Round 16, production art (M4 part C batch). Review sheet (`.png`, no loop: the chunks are static) made from the final files in `assets/` by `tools/art/vrell_l03.py --review`; the chunks themselves are rendered by `tools/art/vrell_l03.py` (see `tools/art/README.md`). Shows Level 03's debris-field wreck chunks, enlarged and at 1×: the indestructible `debris-large-a` (96×80, a frigate hull section broken at the bow, torn open so its ring frames and deck show), `debris-large-b` (72×64, half a CDF defence platform with its array wing) and `debris-large-c` (56×48, a habitat module with a truss stub), and the breakable `debris-small-a` (32×28, a bent hull plate) and `debris-small-b` (24×24, a truss junction). They are not Vrell: they are the Earth-orbit station kit (`tools/concept/render/station.py` materials in Level 01's muted kit palette `KIT_PAL`, the kit's frigate section, platform and drum), broken along jagged cuts, hollow where torn, the lights dead, dulled by soot and scorched along the breaks and in a few blast spots; each is posed in the model under the fixed key light (the game draws them unrotated) and centred in its sprite; one 32-colour palette for the five. Not an image-generator prompt: the brief for reviewing the production chunks.
