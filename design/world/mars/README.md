# Mars

## Summary

Mars under siege: red canyons, terraforming domes, dust storms, and Olympus Mons on the
horizon. Setting for Act 3 — where the Vrell mysteriously spare every Helix facility. Ends with
the Dust Colossus.

## Design

### Look & feel

Hot colours, cold air. Rust-red deserts, deep canyons of Valles Marineris, green terraforming
domes, atmosphere processors pumping plumes into a butterscotch sky. Vrell here burrow: the
ground itself heaves. Touchstones: *Total Recall* (1990), Red Faction, Tyrian's desert stages.

### Sub-locations

| Sub-location | Description |
|---|---|
| Ares Landing | The oldest colony: domes, greenhouses, rail lines, spaceport. |
| Tharsis Foundry | Industrial colony on the Tharsis plateau: smelters, conveyor lines, furnace vents; built with Helix Dynamics money. |
| Valles Marineris | Flying through the canyon: walls on both sides, bridges, cliff-side habitats. |
| Terraforming fields | Atmosphere processors, ice-mining craters, pipelines. |
| Helix compound | A gleaming black-and-gold research site, untouched by the Vrell (story hint). |
| Tharsis dunes | Open dune sea at the foot of Olympus Mons. |
| Hellas basin | A vast impact basin in the south: the retreat along its rim and the Dust Colossus rising from its floor. |

### Parallax layers

| Layer | Contents |
|---|---|
| deep | Canyon floor far below, dune seas, Olympus Mons silhouette. |
| ground | Domes, pipelines, rail, turrets, burrow holes, canyon ledges. |
| low-air | Dust devils, canyon walls (framing the sides), rovers on bridges. |
| air | Player's plane. |
| high-air | Dust storm fronts, canyon arches passing over the player. |

### Palette & lighting

- Rust red, ochre and brown; butterscotch sky haze; small, pale sun from top-left.
- Domes glow green inside; Coalition structures dusty grey-white.
- Dust storms desaturate everything to orange-brown; Vrell teal glow cuts through the haze.

### Hazards & set pieces

- **Canyon walls**: in Valles Marineris, the walls narrow the flyable area; touching them
  damages. (Signature set piece.)
- **Burrowers**: the ground bulges before a Vrell burrower erupts — watch for the tell.
- **Dust storm**: visibility drops; enemies appear late; homing weapons get an edge.
- **Dust devils**: rotating columns that push the player sideways.

### Natives

- Vrell: burrowers, sand-swimmers, ground artillery organisms, dust-camouflaged flyers.
- Coalition: domes and processors to protect, militia defences.
- Helix/Ascendancy: the untouched compound — not hostile yet, but suspicious.

### Music mood

Mysterious and dry. Tribal percussion, detuned synths, a desert-wind drone; a lonely lead
melody.
