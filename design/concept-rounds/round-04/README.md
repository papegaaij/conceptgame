# Concept round 04 — enemy colours, portrait colour, beam sounds

## Summary

Follow-ups from [round 03](../round-03/README.md): a colour differentiation pass on the chosen
enemies, the style-B portraits with more colour retained, and new beam loops plus beam
start/stop, sonar pulses and extra huge and under-water explosions. Open
[index.html](index.html) in a browser (regenerate with `python3 tools/concept/board.py 04`).
