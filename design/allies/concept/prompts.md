# Allies – concept prompts

## Civilian crawler (round 16)

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/allies_r16.py](../../../tools/concept/allies_r16.py)
(`python3 tools/concept/allies_r16.py [a] [b] [c]`). Each sheet shows the crawler on the chosen
Luna scene's regolith ([scene-luna-r06-a](../../art-direction/concept/scene-luna-r06-a.png)) with
a graded road, a column of five at 1× (60 px apart as in Level 04; C at 84 px, because 72 px long
crawlers 60 px apart would overlap), a 3× close-up, the damaged look (scorch marks and a smoke
trail, below 50 % HP) and the wrecked look (stopped, charred, lights out, small fires and a dark
smoke column) at 2×, and all three states at 1×.

Shared direction: an SDF ray-marched model in the station kit look (UTC/CDF pale blue-white hull
plates, navy frames, muted beige cargo pods: Level 01's `KIT_PAL`), a CDF blue stripe, lit yellow
windows, and small **emissive amber hazard beacons** with a soft glow so the crawler reads as
friendly against grey regolith and teal/lime Vrell. No matte amber on the hull: on the ground
layer that is reserved for loot targets (art direction, readability rule 7). Top-down view, fixed
point key light at the top-left, shadow down-right.

Common negative prompt: `perspective view, isometric, military tank, gun turret, weapons, camouflage,
cyan or teal glow, green lights, matte orange hazard stripes, photographic, modern PBR render,
watermark, text`

### civilian-crawler-r16-a

Tracked crawler-transporter, 72×40 px (wide reading). Prompt: `late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game
sprite, strict top-down view, a wide low civilian crawler-transporter on the Moon like NASA's
crawler-transporter, a flat pale blue-white deck on four navy twin-track trucks at the corners,
a pressurised white passenger drum lying across the deck with two blue stripe rings and a row of
small yellow lit windows, a row of beige cargo pods along the rear edge, a small cab with a
glass visor at the front right, radiator and exhaust stacks, glowing amber hazard beacons at the
four deck corners, glossy metal highlights from a point light at the top-left, crisp
palette-limited pixel edges, on grey lunar regolith with long black shadows`

### civilian-crawler-r16-b

Six-wheeled rover train, 72×40 px (wide reading). Prompt: `late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game
sprite, strict top-down view, a civilian lunar rover train of three pressurised white cylinders
side by side, the outer two passenger cars with blue stripe rings and rows of yellow lit
windows, the centre cab car with a blue glass dome nose and a roof radiator, coupled by navy
frame bars, three big dark balloon wheels down each outer side, glowing amber hazard beacons at
the front and back of each car and on the cab roof, glossy metal highlights from a point light
at the top-left, crisp palette-limited pixel edges, on grey lunar regolith with long black
shadows`

### civilian-crawler-r16-c

Pressurised bus with a cargo sled, 40×72 px (long reading, the alternative to A and B).
Prompt: `late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, strict top-down view, a long civilian lunar
transport: a pressurised white bus with rounded corners, a blue roof stripe, two rows of yellow
lit windows along the roof edges and a glass nose, six dark wheels, towing on a short tow bar a
navy sled on skids loaded with four strapped beige cargo pods, glowing amber hazard beacons at
the bus roof corners and the sled's rear corners, glossy metal highlights from a point light at
the top-left, crisp palette-limited pixel edges, on grey lunar regolith with long black shadows`

## civilian-crawler-final-r17-a

Round 17, production art (M4 part D batch). Review sheet (`.png`) and loop (`.gif`) made from the final files in `assets/` by `tools/art/civilian_crawler.py --review`; the frames themselves are rendered by `tools/art/civilian_crawler.py` (see `tools/art/README.md`) from the chosen variant c model of `tools/concept/allies_r16.py`, imported unchanged. Three sets:

- `civilian-crawler_0..20` (72×84 canvas, the 40×72 crawler centred, driving up the screen, 40 colours): 7 headings × 3 wheel frames, indexed heading × 3 + phase. Heading k is turned −30 + 10k degrees clockwise on screen from straight up (index 0 = nose to the left, 3 = straight up, 6 = nose to the right); each heading is its own render under the fixed key light, the tyre tread evaluated around each wheel's axle in the model's frame and moved 1 px forward per frame.
- `civilian-crawler-wreck_0..6` (72×84, 32 colours): per heading the burnt-out wreck, windows, beacons and lights dead, the hull darkened to 62 % and charred in seven blast spots that turn with it. The fire and smoke are game effects.
- `civilian-crawler-pip` (10×18): the HUD pip, a flat white top-down silhouette (bus with wheel notches, tow bar, four pods), tinted at runtime; the sheet shows it white and tinted green, amber and dark.

The sheet shows the headings at 2×, the wheel frames at 4×, the wrecks at 2×, the pip at 6× and all at 1×; the loop has a column of five at Level 04's spacing (84 px, centres at y = 150 … 486) following a winding road on the Luna regolith of round 16 while the ground scrolls at 120 px/s, each crawler showing the heading nearest the road's direction. The concept's additive glow around the amber beacons is not in the sprites (the beacons themselves are emissive). Not an image-generator prompt: the look is the chosen concept's, whose prompt stays valid; this is the brief for reviewing the production frames.
