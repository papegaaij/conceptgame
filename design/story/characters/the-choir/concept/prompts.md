# The Choir — concept prompts

## portrait-r08-a — Vrell channel interference glyph (round 08)

Generated by `tools/concept/portraits_r08.py` (run `python3 tools/concept/portraits_r08.py choir`, ~5 s).
The Choir has no face: when it bleeds into the radio, the portrait well shows an animated alien
glyph on a violet/teal "Vrell channel". The sheet shows the 144×144 briefing size, the 72×72 HUD
radio size in the HUD A frame and a strip of loop frames; the GIF (`portrait-r08-a.gif`, 32
frames at 12 fps) shows the glyph breathing with the song, the sigil slowly turning and sync
tears. Acts 1–2 subtitle: "[the Choir sings]".

**Prompt:** 1990s game radio pixel portrait, 72×72 pixel art scaled up with hard pixels, an alien
transmission instead of a face: a five-armed bioluminescent sigil of twisting tendrils around a
glowing core, inside faint rippling concentric rings, deep violet to lilac with teal and
white-hot highlights on the brightest strands, dark comm-screen background with a faint dot
grid, ordered dithering, horizontal sync tears and an interference band, CRT scanlines;
eerie, choral, organic, unreadable.

**Negative prompt:** human face, eyes, mouth, skull, text, letters, runes from real alphabets, symmetric corporate logo, smooth vector gradients, photorealism, 3D render, HD detail, watermark

**Animation brief:** 32-frame seamless loop at 12 fps; the song swells twice per loop (core and
tendrils brighten, rings tighten), the sigil turns about 1/5 of a turn per loop, 2–4 rows tear
sideways per frame, a bright noise line every fourth frame, an interference band rolling down.
In game the swell can be driven by the Choir's audio level.
