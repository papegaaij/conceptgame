# Dr. Elena Varga

## Summary

Aegis Wing's intel officer and the Coalition's leading xenobiologist. She explains the enemy:
weak points, behaviours, what is coming in the next level. The first to suspect the
Ascendancy.

## Design

- **Role**: voice of the hangar intel panel (see [ui](../../../ui/README.md)); radio lines on
  new enemy types and bosses ("hit the glowing sacs"); occasional mission briefings.
- **Personality**: brilliant, curious, a little too fascinated by the Vrell. Talks fast when
  excited. Speaks truth to authority, then gets overruled.
- **Voice**: precise, scientific, sometimes forgets there is a war on ("Beautiful. Also
  deadly. Mostly deadly.").
- **Background**: 40s, Hungarian, worked on Helix-funded gate research until 2179 — she left
  after the "missing" survey mission and never believed the accident story.
- **Arc**: notices in Act 3 that the Vrell avoid Helix sites; Okafor tells her to keep quiet.
  In Act 4 she decodes the Helix frequencies on the beacons. In Act 7 she is the one who
  understands the Choir's fear and hints at the Silence.
- **Gameplay tie-in**: each level's intel line from Varga names one practical tip (weak point,
  recommended weapon trait, dominant attack direction), consistent with the level's threat
  profile.

### Portrait brief

White woman in her forties, dark auburn hair pinned up with a stylus through it, thin wire
glasses, intense eyes. Lab coat over a CDF intelligence uniform, a holographic display casting
green light on her face. Neutral: focused; alternates: excited (half-smile, leaning to the
screen), alarmed (hand to mouth). Late-90s pre-rendered CGI style.
