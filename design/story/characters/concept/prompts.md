# Briefing cast — lineup prompts

Cast sheets generated by `tools/concept/portraits_r03.py` (run `python3 tools/concept/portraits_r03.py`, ~40 s); per-character prompts are in each character's
`concept/prompts.md` ([Okafor](../okafor/concept/prompts.md), [Rook](../rook/concept/prompts.md),
[Varga](../varga/concept/prompts.md), [Vorne](../vorne/concept/prompts.md)).

## cast-r03-a — style A lineup

**Prompt:** Character lineup of four late-1990s pre-rendered CGI briefing portraits side by side,
same camera, same framing, three-quarter view, each on its own tinted comm-screen background
(blue for the commander, amber for the pilot, green for the intelligence officer, black and gold
for the villain), coloured rim lights, soft scanlines, 3D Studio MAX 1998 look. Commander: Black
woman in her fifties, grey cropped hair, navy uniform, headset. Pilot: Japanese man, messy black
hair, pushed-up flight helmet, crooked grin, orange shoulder patch. Scientist: auburn bun with a
stylus, round wire glasses, white lab coat. Villain: slicked silver hair, trimmed beard, black
high-collared suit with gold embroidery, teal glow at the temple.

**Negative prompt:** photorealistic photo, modern Unreal/Octane render, subsurface-scattering realism, anime, cartoon, chibi, painterly brush strokes, watermark, text, logo, extra fingers, hands, full body, busy background, depth-of-field blur, lens flare spam, 4k gloss, deformed face, asymmetric eyes

## cast-r03-b — style B lineup

**Prompt:** Four 1990s game radio pixel portraits side by side, 72×72 pixel art scaled up with
hard pixels, monochrome teal comm-screen palette with ordered dithering for the three Coalition
officers and amber-gold for the enemy chairman, 1-pixel outlines, interference lines, CRT
scanlines; same four characters as above.

**Negative prompt:** smooth gradients, photorealism, 3D render, anti-aliased vector art, high resolution detail, full colour, anime, chibi, text, watermark, logo, hands, full body, clean HD look

## cast-r04-a — style B with retained colour (round 04, current direction)

Generated by `tools/concept/portraits_r04.py` (run `python3 tools/concept/portraits_r04.py`, ~35 s).

**Prompt:** Four 1990s game radio pixel portraits side by side, 72×72 pixel art scaled up with
hard pixels, 32–40 colours each: every character keeps their own skin, hair and uniform colours
under a translucent teal comm-screen tint (amber for the enemy chairman), soft ordered
dithering, 1-pixel outlines, interference band, CRT scanlines; commander (dark skin, grey
cropped hair, navy uniform, headset), pilot (black hair, pushed-up helmet, orange shoulder
patch), scientist (auburn bun with stylus, wire glasses, white lab coat), villain (silver hair
and beard, black suit with gold, teal temple glow).

**Negative prompt:** smooth gradients, photorealism, 3D render, anti-aliased vector art, HD detail, fully monochrome image, flat single-hue portrait, heavy noisy dithering, anime, chibi, text, watermark, logo, hands, full body, clean modern look
