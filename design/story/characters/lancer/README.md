# Lancer

## Summary

The player character: a young pilot of the CDF 7th Interceptor Wing, "Aegis Wing", callsign
"Lancer", flying the AF-12 Stormhawk. A silent protagonist — the player's skill is the
character.

## Design

- **Name, gender, face**: never shown or spoken. Only the callsign is used. No portrait; the
  ship is the character (see [player](../../../player/README.md)).
- **Background** (implied through dialogue): top of the class, fresh out of training when the
  war starts; assigned to the brand-new Stormhawk because nobody else has flown it yet.
- **How others treat Lancer**:
  - Okafor: starts formal and protective, becomes trusting. By Act 6 she sends Lancer alone on
    missions she would give nobody else.
  - Rook: teasing older-brother energy; worried about Lancer more than he admits.
  - Varga: talks to Lancer as the only one who listens to her theories.
  - Vorne: first ignores, then admires, then hates Lancer. Tries to recruit Lancer in Act 5.
  - The Choir: in Act 7 starts addressing Lancer directly — "…the small bright one…".
- **Arc**: from rookie to the pilot the whole Coalition knows. Reflected in how others speak,
  not in anything Lancer says.

## Decisions

- 2026-09-30: Silent protagonist, callsign only, no portrait.
- 2026-10-01: Approved by the user in the [Acts 1–2 design review](../../../reviews/acts-1-2/README.md).
