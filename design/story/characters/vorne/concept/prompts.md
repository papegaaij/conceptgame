# Chairman Silas Vorne — portrait prompts

Mockups generated by `tools/concept/portraits_r03.py` (run `python3 tools/concept/portraits_r03.py`, ~40 s). The procedural faces only show the **style** (framing, lighting,
colour treatment, frame and overlay); use the prompts below for polished portraits. The final
portrait is 144×144 (briefing) and is reduced to 72×72 for the HUD radio panel, so the face must
read at small size: clear silhouette, strong value contrast, one or two signature props.

Character: Tall, lean man who looks forty but has old eyes; slicked-back silver hair, sharp cheekbones, neatly trimmed grey beard, faint knowing smile, head tilted slightly back as if looking down on the viewer. High-collared black suit with gold embroidery and the Ascendancy crest (a rising sun over a ringed planet) in gold on the lapel. A faint teal bioluminescent shimmer under the skin at his temple (Vrell biotech).

Lighting / colour: Black background with warm golden rim light. Enemy transmission: in style B his channel is amber instead of teal.
