# United Terran Coalition (UTC)

## Summary

The union of Earth, Luna, Mars, Europa and the belt stations, founded in 2102. The player's
side. Its military is the Coalition Defence Force (CDF): capable, underfunded, and built for
policing, not for an alien war.

## Design

### Goals and culture

- Keep humanity together and alive. After the first weeks of war, nothing else matters.
- Bureaucratic, earnest, proud of two centuries of expansion. Earth-centric: the colonies
  (especially Jupiter) feel talked down to, which is the root of the Ascendancy.
- Military culture: practical, dry humour, callsigns, lots of acronyms.

### Visual language

- **Ships and structures**: blocky, modular, functional. Visible panel lines, hatches, stencilled
  hull numbers, hazard stripes. Think industrial aerospace.
- **Colours**: light grey and off-white hulls, navy blue, hazard orange accents, CDF roundel
  (a blue ring around a white chevron).
- **Engines**: blue-white fusion flame, clean and bright.
- **Cities and bases**: dense, lit, layered — highways, landing pads, radar dishes, domes.

### Military (CDF)

| Unit | Role in the game |
|---|---|
| 7th Interceptor Wing "Aegis Wing" | The player's unit. Lancer and wingman Rook. |
| CDF capital ships (carriers, frigates) | Allies seen on the background layers; escort targets; supply the hangar between levels. |
| Ground defences | Friendly turrets and AA sites, sometimes to protect, sometimes already overrun. |
| Airstrike wings | The source of the player's airstrike special ability. |

Allied ships appear as background set pieces and escort objectives; they are not controlled by
the player (except the AI wingman, see [player](../../../player/README.md)).

### Doctrine (as seen in-game)

- Defensive: hold lines, protect evacuations, escort convoys. Many levels have an allied asset
  in the background that the player is protecting.
- Improvised: as the war drags on, Coalition hardware gets patched, repainted and scavenged —
  the player's upgrades are partly salvaged Vrell and Ascendancy tech (see Act 5+).
