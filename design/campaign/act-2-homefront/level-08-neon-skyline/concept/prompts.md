# Level 08 – Neon Skyline: concept prompts and briefs

## billboard

The billboard secret, "LAGOS NEVER SLEEPS" (concept round 30, two variants; user decision D6 = a of
M5 part B: a/b only for the level's new props). A ground trigger on a flat low roof in the Rooftop
Crawl (t≈100; drawn without lean, D1 = a): it flickers (the secret's blink), takes 3 hits and
topples, and its hidden CDF crate drops (the crate pickup). It follows readability rule 7 of the
[art direction](../../../../art-direction/README.md#readability-rules): matte amber/black hazard
markings against the cool steel, a 1 px light rim, a visible damage state from the first hit, a
clear break (the topple); the glint, the hit flash and the spark are the game's own sprites. The
neon and the screen avoid the reserved bullet, shot and Vrell hues and never get a bright round
core. Level 08's data has no size for it yet; proposed: sprite 76×52 (the shadow (5, 7) baked
in), hit box 64×36 (the panel), frames `billboard_0..2` (intact, hit, toppled) and an additive
flicker `billboard-glow_0..7` (0–3 intact: lit, buzz, dim, off; 4–7 the same after a hit) cycled
16 steps at 8 fps, which needs a renderer addition (a trigger's `-glow` is one still frame today).
Both sheets are generated by `python3 tools/concept/props_r30.py billboard [a] [b]`: top-down SDF
renders (render/sdf.py, the fixed top-left key light, 1 world unit = 1 px, 6× supersampled, 32
colours) with the loot amber, light rim and scorch of ground_targets.py; the lettering and the
screen are 2D pixel art on the panel's projected face, the glows 2D light fields added after the
sprite. Each sheet shows the frames and glows at 1×, 3× and 2×, both flicker cycles, and three 1×
play-field frames over the chosen megacity
([parallax-r03-b](../../../../art-direction/concept/parallax-r03-b.png), atmosphere `light`)
with the production player ship (a burst passing beside it, the first hit with its flash and
spark, toppled with the crate drifting free) plus a 2× crop after the second hit. The GIF plays
5.2 s.

Outcome of round 30 (user, 2026-10-07): **a** chosen, the neon sky-sign; b moved to `rejected/`
(the heading below keeps its file names). Its production sprites: `billboard-final-r30-a` below.

## traffic

The low-air traffic over the avenues (concept round 30, two variants; user decision D2 = a of M5
part B: scenery, no collision, shots pass through, so it must never read as a target): civilian
aircars fleeing south in streams and CDF craft heading north. Muted below the play plane
(82 % brightness, 75 % saturation; rule 3 of the
[art direction](../../../../art-direction/README.md#readability-rules)), no rim, no glint, no hit
reaction, no loot amber and no enemy hues; lit only by small headlights, red tail lights, nav lights
and a strobe, with small additive halos; the low-air shadow (9, 13). Level 08's data has no sizes
yet; proposed: `low-air` pieces with `headings: 16` (`_0` up the screen, clockwise, as the
backdrop's angle sets), streams as placed pieces with a `repeat` (a car about every 0.37 s per
southbound lane), own speed 111 px/s south (300 px/s on the screen with the layer's 189) and
109 px/s north (80 on the screen), inside the 120 px/s motion budget. Both sheets are generated by
`python3 tools/concept/props_r30.py traffic [a] [b]`: top-down SDF renders (render/sdf.py, the fixed
top-left key light, 1 world unit = 1 px, 8× supersampled, 32 colours), each heading a render from
the model. Each sheet shows the craft at 1× heading up and down (the cars in several paints), five
of the 16 headings at 3×, and three 1× play-field frames over the chosen megacity
([parallax-r03-b](../../../../art-direction/concept/parallax-r03-b.png); atmosphere `clear`,
`light`, `light`) with the production player ship and Skitters: the vulcan stream passes over the
cars and kills Skitters; plus a 2× crop of shots over a stream. The GIF plays 4 s.

## backdrop-final-r30-a

The Level 08 backdrop, Nova Lagos at night, as production art (user decision D6 = a of M5 part B:
straight to production from the chosen megacity, [parallax-r03-b](../../../../art-direction/concept/parallax-r03-b.png),
extended in the same kit to the harbour, the elevated highways, the smoke district and Neon
Heights). Generator: `python3 tools/art/backdrop_l08.py` (images into `assets/backdrop/level-08/`,
`--review` this sheet, `--check` the seams, the motion budget and the tower placement, `--proposal`
the backdrop block for the level's data (merged into it on 2026-10-06; once merged the generator reads the level's own block), `--strip t,.. out.png [--units]` full-size composites).
It imports the frozen concept kit unchanged (`parallax_r03`'s fog banks and tree canopies,
`render/`), palette B (UI PANELS navy for the city, sodium pools from MARS, windows in JOVIAN and
EARTH ORBIT, trees in EARTH SURFACE at night, neon in the UTC ACCENTS reds and ambers, no teal),
the top-left key light; flat structures are height fields lit with cast shadows, rendered at 2–4×
and box-reduced; 12–32 colours per image, translucency stepped.

Towers (D1 = a): pre-rendered roofs at their drawn size (the footprint × 6 ÷ (6 − h), h 0.35–1.35,
styles city a/b, burnt, neon a/b) and wall textures per style and height class, projected by the
game round the play field's (240, 297). A tower stands only on a city lot and only where its walls
and roof keep clear of every Creeper unit's path, every ground target and its structure while on
screen; the targets' structures and the Creepers' walk roofs (a low roof and a ramp per walker
wave, repeating every 1.5 s for a convoy) are sized from the level's data, drawn flat. The Ndidi
Arcology (h 1.5, creep on its roof and upper floors) rises at the level's end. The aircar and
gunship pieces were plain placeholders until round 30 chose the traffic (a); the sheet was rebuilt
with the production traffic ([traffic-final-r30-a](#traffic-final-r30-a)).

**Readability pass** (2026-10-07, after the capture below; before/after in
[level-08-readability-r30-b](#level-08-readability-r30-b)). The smoke banks part over the ground
units: the Creepers' places, the stretches they have walked (where their husks lie) and the ground
targets' boxes are known at every moment, so `bank_parting` maps them, at every 0.1 s the bank is
drawn (weighted by its share of the look), into the bank tile's own rows and columns (the low-air
scroll, the sideways drift) and lowers the bank's noise there by a depth (34 px round a unit, an
18 px soft edge), so the bank opens with its own billowy edges and the tile still wraps both ways.
The heavy smoke wall (t 166–174) parts fully over the Third Mainland convoy and its husks (noise
depth 0.34) and is a little thinner (most alpha 165 instead of 190), so it covers about 35 % of
its tile instead of 59 % (the art direction's heavy end is 25–40 %); the medium smoke thins over
the units (depth 0.13, husk tracks at half weight) and is a little denser elsewhere (cover 0.57
instead of 0.62), about 19 % instead of 27 %. The smoke plumes pick, of their four columns, the one
that veils the ground units least (`plume_overlap`): the t 130.5 plume moved from x 333 (over the
t 128 Creepers' path) to x 93. The landing pad is pale concrete (`3e4460`) instead of the dark road
tone, and every turret of the landing pad, the roof nest and the parking deck stands on a pale
painted spot (`969cb2`, 17 px radius) with a dark seam, a pale painted ring and four small
cool-white lamps (`turret_spots`), so the dark Spine Turrets read as silhouettes; no amber (the
loot targets' hue on the ground).

- **Prompt (artist brief):** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI shoot'em-up backdrop, straight top-down,
  a West African megacity at night in 2185: dark navy city blocks with flat low roofs, rooftop
  gardens and water tanks, tree-lined avenues with amber sodium lamp pools, an elevated six-lane
  highway, a harbour with container yards, gantry cranes and a moored container ship, glass
  towers seen in true perspective from above with sparse lit windows, a smoke district of
  collapsed blocks with embers and smoke plumes, rooftop neon signs in red and amber, a giant
  arcology overgrown on top by violet alien growth; key light from the top-left, low contrast
  against the play plane, posterized palette, no text but the signs.

**Contrast measure.** Relative luminance L (linear sRGB), contrast (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05).
*Silhouette*: the unit's outermost pixel row against the pixels just outside it (2 px); *mean*: the
unit's pixels against a 10 px ring round it. Measured on the backdrop as the game draws it
(`backdrop_l08.composite`) with the units drawn at their path places under the low-air layer (the
Creepers' glow masks added above it), so bullets, shots and hit flashes stay out; and checked in
the game's frames (the predicted unit masks aligned to the pixels that differ from the backdrop,
shots and bullets left out; units hit or killed in one run are skipped).
