# Level 07 – Brood Carrier: concept prompts and briefs

## backdrop-final-r25-a

Production art, round 25 (`backdrop-final-r25-a.png`, straight to production as part F's D8: the
Earth-orbit setting has its chosen scene, parallax r03 A): not prompted, rendered by
`python3 tools/art/backdrop_l07.py` from the level's `backdrop` block (`backdrop-proposal.yaml` until
it is merged); `--review` rebuilds only the sheet. The sheet shows every tile set and set piece (the
carrier's two headings, the fleet line's four frames at 2×) and twelve composites of the level at
half scale, drawn as the game's Backdrop draws them (deep, far, haze, ground, low-air banks and
pieces, high-air additive at 40 %), from the picket line to the second fleet in the aftermath.

## lifeboat

The lifeboat tow secret, Lifeboat Seven (concept round 25, two variants; the level's data `tows`:
boat 72×36, pod 32×32 hanging 70 px above (behind) the boat's centre, the cable's hit box 16×40
midway, 3 hits). A friendly CDF lifeboat on the air layer: shots, bullets and the ship pass it, so
it must read as a human rescue craft and not as a target; only the cable takes hits, and when it
is cut the pod falls free as the hidden crate (the crate pickup). Both sheets are generated by
`python3 tools/concept/props_r25.py [a] [b]`: top-down SDF renders (render/sdf.py, the fixed
top-left key light, 1 world unit = 1 px, 8× supersampled up to 64 px and 4× above, 32 colours)
in palette B's UTC hull and accents, with the loot amber under black hazard stripes of
ground_targets.py; strobes, marker lamps, the light strip and the glint are additive glows added
after the sprites. Each sheet shows the parts at 1× and 3× (the boat with its strobe lit and dark,
the pod, the cable intact, after 1 and 2 hits and cut), the tow assembled as the game's TowLooks
lays it out, and three 1× play-field frames over Level 07's own production backdrop with the
production player ship (a vulcan burst passing the boat, the first hit's glance spark, the cut with
the crate falling free) plus a 2× crop of a shot crossing the boat. The GIF plays t=22.6–32.6 s at
real speed. Sprites as frames: `lifeboat` (strobe lit, dark), `lifeboat-pod`, `lifeboat-cable`
(intact, 1 hit, 2 hits, cut).

### lifeboat-r25-a

Rejected (user picked b); the files are in `rejected/`.

Variant A "white rescue boat": a white lifting-body lifeboat, nose down the way it drifts, with
international-orange outer wings and nose band, a CDF blue band, four lit cabin windows (crew of
four), a green strobe on a short mast, red/green nav lights at the wingtips, a dark engine block
with two dead nozzles and a tow eye aft. The pod is a plain olive CDF canister with a white stencil
plate and a small hazard tag (deliberately not a target). The cable is a braided steel tether with
three amber marker lamps that blink and short hazard sleeves: the only target cue. Each hit puts a
lamp out and frays strands (a count-down), the cut leaves a frayed stub hanging from the boat.

- **Prompt:** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, straight top-down orthographic, key light from
  the top-left, a small white space lifeboat shaped like a stubby lifting body with orange rescue
  wingtips and a blue stripe, a row of four lit cabin windows, a green strobe light on top, towing an
  olive cargo canister on a braided steel cable with three amber marker lamps, crisp silhouette,
  isolated on a flat dark background.

### lifeboat-r25-b

Variant B "orange lifeboat capsule": a rescue-orange pressure capsule lying across the boat's box,
white end caps and bands, a CDF blue band, a docking collar and a thruster bell at the ends, four
lit portholes, two blue strobes, a handrail, a radiator fin and a tow bridle from two hard points.
The pod is a square crate-pod with an amber/black hazard lid, dark corner posts and a light rim (it
reads as loot). The cable is thin with an amber light strip and a hazard-striped breakaway coupler
in the middle, where the hits land: the first dents it and darkens the strip above it, the second
splits it, the cut leaves its lower jaw hanging from the boat.

- **Prompt:** as A, but a bright orange cylindrical lifeboat capsule with white end caps, lit
  portholes and two blue strobes, towing a square cargo pod with a black-and-amber hazard lid on a
  thin cable with a glowing amber light strip and a hazard-striped breakaway coupler in the middle.

## level-07-capture-final-r25-a

Round 25, a capture of the game, not generated art (`.png` sheet, `.mp4` with the game's sound),
taken in M4 part G and retaken after the round's fixes (the carrier at 75 % on high air, launches
drawn over the hull, homing on the overhead pass, the steady hit tint, the carcass and the fleet
line in the aftermath, the lifeboat pod tumbling away with the crate falling out low, the master
limiter): `desktop/build/install/terran-vanguard/bin/terran-vanguard --bench 380 --settings <file>
--level 7 --invulnerable --loadout
front=lance-laser:4,left=micro-missile-pod:4,right=micro-missile-pod:4` at medium on a private Xvfb
display (960×540; settings: a 960×540 window at 0,0, `controls.auto-fire=true`), the atlases packed
from the current `assets/` by `:desktop:installDist`. Video: `ffmpeg -f x11grab -draw_mouse 0
-framerate 30 -copyts` (wall-clock timestamps); sound: OpenAL Soft's `wave` driver (`ALSOFT_CONF`
with `drivers = wave`) writing the game's mix to a file, nothing played aloud (the game logged
`audio: output limiter on`), aligned to the video by the file's end time and checked against the
klaxon (found by a matched filter at t 99.95, the banner's first frame at t 99.95). The ship was
flown by XTest key events on that display (a small Python driver, dead reckoning at 270 px/s from
the start position, reset against the right wall at t 127 and the left wall when phase 3 starts,
since many short key presses drift it): under the lifeboat's cable from t 25, under the loose pod
(x 282) from t 26.4 to 33, at the centre through the approach and the overhead phase, then from the
turn on under the bay pair whose sacs it saw open on screen (yellow-green, not the cyan lance beam;
it stays on a pair while it is open, then the next pair in hull order between windows), under the
core once no sac had opened for 6 s; Enter pressed through the debrief, the act summary and the four
outro pages. Level time t = the level's clock, synchronised on the boss banner; after the kill (the
screen flash at the chain's end, t 162.53) the frames are labelled kill + s, since the clock jumps to
the aftermath. The sheet's thirteenth play field is a 1:1 crop around the ship under the hull. The
mp4 is the play field: the tow from t 21.5 (8.5 s, to the crate's pick-up), then t 98.5 to kill + 8 s
(80.6 s in all).
The run: the cable cut at about t 25.8 (3 hits from t 25.6), the pod tumbling aside, the crate out of it
at t 28.4 about 160 px above the bottom edge and collected by t 29.0 (+75); phase 1 no sac burst
(BAYS 0 / 8 at the turn) but the boss bar down to 77 % from the missiles on the open sacs (8 % in
the first capture); phase 2 all eight sacs out by about t 145 (secondary met), the iris at bar + 45,
the core destroyed at bar + 59.55 (BOSS TIME 0:59), the 3 s chain and its flash at bar + 62.5; the
crack glow gone by kill + 12 s, the dark carcass on screen to the debrief, the fleet line from
kill + 10 s (behind the carcass first). Debrief: 153 / 165 kills, SECRETS FOUND 1 / 1 with 75 secret
credits, 2 143 total credits, A+ (rating 97). The mix's peak is 1.000 (52 samples at full scale, in
runs of at most 2 samples; the first capture peaked at 1.46).
