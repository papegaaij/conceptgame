# Level 06 – Farside: concept prompts and briefs

All round-23 prop sheets are generated by `python3 tools/concept/props_r23.py [ore-cart]
[survey-cache] [data-core]`: top-down SDF renders (render/sdf.py, fixed top-left key light, 1 world
unit = 1 px, 4× supersampled, 32 colours) with the station kit's materials and the loot amber under
black hazard stripes (ground_targets_r17.py, ground_targets.py's light rim and scorch). Each sheet
shows the frames at 1× and 3× on lit regolith and, at 2×, in context on the production Luna ground
(Level 05's mass-driver field and mare tiles) under a light map built like the game's darkness
(cool ambient, warm lamp or dome pool, the cool headlight cone, a flare), glows added after it.

Outcome of round 23 (user, 2026-10-05): ore cart **b**, survey cache **a**, data core **b** chosen;
ore cart a, survey cache b and data core a moved to `rejected/` (the headings below keep the
files' names). Their production sprites: `l06-props-final-r23-a` below.

## ore-cart-r23-a

The abandoned ore cart on the rail (proposed 28×40, ground layer, 3 HP, medium salvage 50), variant
A: an open rust ore tub heaped with grey-brown ore, an amber/black hazard rim, four wheels on the
rail and couplers at both ends; intact, damaged (dent, scorch, less ore), wrecked (tipped open, ore
spilled, amber burnt dull, no rim).

- **Prompt:** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, straight top-down orthographic, key light from
  the top-left, a small abandoned lunar mine cart on a rail, open rust-red ore tub heaped with grey
  ore, amber and black hazard-striped rim, crisp silhouette, isolated on a flat dark background.

## ore-cart-r23-b

Variant B: a closed hopper car, steel body with an olive roof and two amber/black hatched lid panels,
a red beacon at one corner, couplers; damaged (dents, scorch), wrecked (the top blown open on a dark
interior with ore lumps, a lid panel thrown aside, the body charred).

- **Prompt:** as A, but a closed steel hopper ore car with two hazard-striped hatches on its roof and
  a small red beacon lamp.

## survey-cache-r23-a

The CDF survey cache in the dark crater (proposed 32×24, hidden crate 135, 3 hits, hittable only while
lit), variant A: a ribbed olive CDF field case with amber/black end bands and three retro-reflector
markers in a row on the lid. The markers are plain metal in the dark and **glint only when light hits
them** (a glow added while the headlight or a flare covers the cache), so the secret stays invisible
until lit. Closed, hit, opened (the lid blown off: three instrument tubes and an amber module inside).

- **Prompt:** top-down pre-rendered CGI sprite, a ruggedized military survey case, ribbed olive-drab
  lid, amber and black hazard bands at both ends, three small round reflective markers on the lid,
  key light from the top-left.

## survey-cache-r23-b

Variant B: a half-buried instrument drum in a regolith skirt, a round olive lid in an amber/black
hazard ring, three amber blinkers in a triangle and a folded antenna. The blinkers glow **dimly in the
dark** (55 %, blinking): a faint hint for a sharp-eyed player, at odds with "invisible until lit".
Opened: the lid thrown aside, an instrument block and a sensor head inside.

- **Prompt:** top-down pre-rendered CGI sprite, a cylindrical survey instrument canister half-buried
  in grey regolith, round olive lid with a hazard-striped ring, three tiny amber indicator lights,
  a folded antenna, key light from the top-left.

## data-core-r23-a

The data core at the open airlock of Daedalus Gate: the terminal (proposed 40×32, 2 hits) and the
released core (16×16 pickup), variant A: a steel pedestal console with a slanted teal screen, a keypad
row and an amber/black hazard-striped core bay holding a glowing cyan cartridge; after 2 hits the
bay is open and empty and the screen dark; the core is a cyan data cartridge with an amber grip, with
a stepped cyan glow added after the light pass so it reads as a pickup.

- **Prompt:** top-down pre-rendered CGI sprite, a steel airlock control console with a glowing teal
  screen, a keypad and a hazard-striped bay holding a glowing cyan data cartridge, key light from the
  top-left; separately the cartridge itself.

## data-core-r23-b

Variant B: a long wall cabinet beside the airlock with two rows of amber LEDs, a dark panel and a
round socket in an amber/black ring holding a glowing amber core orb; released: the socket empty, the
LEDs dead; the core an amber orb in a dark equatorial ring with one rib, with a stepped amber glow.

- **Prompt:** top-down pre-rendered CGI sprite, a wall-mounted steel data cabinet with rows of amber
  status lights and a round hazard-ringed socket holding a glowing amber memory orb, key light from
  the top-left; separately the orb in its ring.
