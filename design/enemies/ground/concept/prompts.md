## spine-turret-r03-a

**Spine Turret, language A** — grown turret: smooth bulb with a petal collar and one long thorn barrel.

- **Prompt:** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, 3D model rendered straight top-down orthographic, key light from the top-left, glossy specular highlights, crisp silhouette, isolated on a flat dark background, like Raptor: Call of the Shadows or Tyrian 2000. sleek biomechanical alien, smooth elongated glossy violet chitin (#a020a8 to #d050d0, deep shadows #40004a), thin glowing teal (#00ff9a) seam along the spine, bright pink (#ff40ff) glowing eye as weak point. A smooth glossy bulb rooted into a metal station hull, a collar of chitin petals, one long thorn barrel, roots spreading over the hull. Facing down (towards the bottom of the image). Designed to be downscaled to a 36×36 sprite, so a bold readable silhouette matters more than fine detail.
- **Negative prompt:** photograph, painterly brush strokes, soft focus, perspective camera, three-quarter view, side view, text, watermark, busy background, anti-aliased soft edges, motion blur.
- **Mockup generator:** `python3 tools/concept/enemies_r03.py spine-turret` (models in `tools/concept/render/enemy_models.py`)

# Round 05 — new archetypes

## scuttler-r05-a

**Scuttler (Vrell six-legged walker)** — a 52 px ground walker with 16 headings × 6 walk frames. Files: `scuttler-r05-a.png` (sheet) and `scuttler-r05-a.gif` (motion in the 480×540 play field).

- **Prompt:** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, 3D model rendered straight top-down orthographic, key light from the top-left, glossy specular highlights, crisp silhouette, isolated on a flat dark background. A six-legged alien crab-like ground walker seen from above: low wide mauve-grey slate carapace (#7c6878, shadows #241a24) with bone spikes along the rim, jointed legs splayed around the body, small head with bone mandibles and lime (#a8ff2a) acid glands; on lunar regolith.
- **Negative prompt:** photograph, painterly brush strokes, soft focus, perspective camera, side view, three-quarter view, text, watermark, motion blur, magenta or hot-pink glow, yellow glow, blue or cyan body colour.
- **Animation brief:** Tripod gait (L1 R2 L3 / R1 L2 R3), 6 frames; the walk phase advances with distance walked so the feet do not slide. Turns to face its path (16 headings) while weaving down the screen; spits lime acid blobs.
- **Mockup generator:** `python3 tools/concept/enemies_r05.py scuttler` (models `tools/concept/render/archetype_models.py`, rigs `tools/concept/render/enemy_rigs.py`)
