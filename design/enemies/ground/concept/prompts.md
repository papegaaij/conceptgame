## scuttler-r08-a — Scuttler, model-space re-render (round 08)

Generated by `tools/concept/rerender_r08.py` (run `python3 tools/concept/rerender_r08.py scuttler`).
Identical design, colours, rig, path and sheet layout to the chosen `scuttler-r05-a`; the only
change is the renderer: every angle set uses `enemy_rigs.ModelSpaceAngleSprites`, so material
patterns (chitin ridges, glowing seams, mottled hide) are evaluated in model space and turn with
the body in the heading grids and the GIF, instead of staying fixed to the screen. The image
prompt, negative prompt and animation brief of `scuttler-r05-a` above still apply unchanged.
