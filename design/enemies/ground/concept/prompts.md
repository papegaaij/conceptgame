# Ground enemies — concept prompts

Prompts for polished versions of the round-03 mockups.

## spine-turret-r03-a

**Spine Turret, language A** — grown turret: smooth bulb with a petal collar and one long thorn barrel.

- **Prompt:** late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, 3D model rendered straight top-down orthographic, key light from the top-left, glossy specular highlights, crisp silhouette, isolated on a flat dark background, like Raptor: Call of the Shadows or Tyrian 2000. sleek biomechanical alien, smooth elongated glossy violet chitin (#a020a8 to #d050d0, deep shadows #40004a), thin glowing teal (#00ff9a) seam along the spine, bright pink (#ff40ff) glowing eye as weak point. A smooth glossy bulb rooted into a metal station hull, a collar of chitin petals, one long thorn barrel, roots spreading over the hull. Facing down (towards the bottom of the image). Designed to be downscaled to a 36×36 sprite, so a bold readable silhouette matters more than fine detail.
- **Negative prompt:** photograph, painterly brush strokes, soft focus, perspective camera, three-quarter view, side view, text, watermark, busy background, anti-aliased soft edges, motion blur.
- **Mockup generator:** `python3 tools/concept/enemies_r03.py spine-turret` (models in `tools/concept/render/enemy_models.py`)
