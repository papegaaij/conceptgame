# Enemies — concept prompts

## lineup-r03-a

**Round-03 lineup** — every round-03 enemy at native scale next to the player ship, on four backgrounds (dark, station hull, lunar regolith, Earth from orbit), then at 2× with names, plus the Brood Carrier at 1/4 scale. A readability and size check, not an asset.

- **Prompt:** not applicable (comparison sheet). For a polished faction sheet: late-1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, 3D model rendered straight top-down orthographic, key light from the top-left, glossy specular highlights, crisp silhouette, isolated on a flat dark background, like Raptor: Call of the Shadows or Tyrian 2000; a lineup of alien biomechanical Vrell creatures (violet chitin, teal and pink glow) next to angular black-and-gold human-built Ascendancy craft and a grey-white UTC fighter, all at the same scale, top-down.
- **Negative prompt:** photograph, painterly brush strokes, soft focus, perspective camera, three-quarter view, side view, text, watermark, busy background, anti-aliased soft edges, motion blur.
- **Mockup generator:** `python3 tools/concept/enemies_r03.py lineup` (models in `tools/concept/render/enemy_models.py`)

## enemy-bullets-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py bullets`: the enemy bullet
set in Vrell and Ascendancy colours — orb (small), large pulsing orb (medium), needle (fast),
hollow ring (burst), homing diamond (shootable), Vrell acid glob / Ascendancy rail slug, Vrell
spore mine / Ascendancy proximity mine, mortar ground marker, and the laser telegraph, charge
and beam — at 1× and zoomed, plus a readability test at 1× over the eight chosen scene loops
with pulse-cannon shots for contrast.

**Prompt:** sprite sheet of enemy bullets for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical shoot'em
up, top-down, transparent background, 8 to 13 pixels each: glowing orbs with a bright white
core, a saturated ring and a 1 px dark outline — hot magenta orbs and sharp yellow thorns for
the alien Vrell, lime acid globs and spiky lime spore mines, hollow spinning rings, spinning
diamond seekers; orange orbs, gold-white flechette slugs, long gold rail slugs and dark
proximity mines with a blinking orange light for the human Ascendancy faction; dashed target
reticles; thin flickering laser telegraph lines and thick white-core laser beams; each bullet
type with its own silhouette, crisp, high contrast, readable on any background.

**Negative prompt:** blue, cyan or pale white bullets (player colours), soft rimless glows,
bullets smaller than 8 px, motion blur, photographic realism, text, watermark.
