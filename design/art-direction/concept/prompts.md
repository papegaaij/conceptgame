# Art direction – concept prompts

Prompts and briefs for polished versions of the round 01 art-direction mockups. Palettes are
generated by [tools/concept/palettes.py](../../../tools/concept/palettes.py), the parallax
scenes (PNG sheets and GIF loops) by
[tools/concept/parallax.py](../../../tools/concept/parallax.py). Regenerate everything with
[tools/concept/make_all.py](../../../tools/concept/make_all.py).

Common negative prompt: `perspective camera, isometric, side-scroller, photo, painterly, blurry,
text, watermark, UI, modern PBR realism, cel shading, anime`

## palette-r01-a

**Cold Military Steel** (brief, for a colour-script artist): desaturated steels and cool blues;
colour reserved for accents, glows and bullets. Enemy shots hot magenta/orange, player shots
blue-white. Vrell violet chitin with teal/pink glow, Ascendancy black and gold. Settings each
get a 6-step ramp from dark background to highlight (hex codes on the sheet).

Prompt (mood frame): `top-down view of a vertical shoot-em-up level, cold desaturated steel-blue
colour grading, grey military interceptor, dark violet alien fighters with teal glowing cores,
hot magenta enemy bullets, 1990s pre-rendered CGI sprites, muted Earth terrain below`

## palette-r01-b

**90s Neon CGI**: saturated, glossy, violet-shadowed chrome; everything glows. Needs strict
bullet-contrast rules because backgrounds are colourful too.

Prompt (mood frame): `top-down view of a vertical shoot-em-up level, saturated neon 1998 CGI
colours, violet shadows, glossy chrome interceptor with cyan accents, magenta alien fighters with
green glow, bright yellow and pink bullets, vivid blue and green terrain below, CD-ROM era`

## palette-r01-c

**Warm Cinematic**: teal shadows, amber light, warm greys – a 90s sci-fi film transferred to
sprites. Orange/teal contrast carries readability.

Prompt (mood frame): `top-down view of a vertical shoot-em-up level, cinematic teal and orange
colour grading, warm grey interceptor with teal accents, bone-brown alien fighters with cyan
glow, orange-red enemy bullets, olive and ochre terrain below, 1990s pre-rendered CGI sprites`

## parallax-r01-a

**Earth orbit**, layer breakdown on the sheet, 4 s seamless loop in the GIF.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic view straight down onto a low Earth orbit battle, 320x360 portrait
game screen, far below: blue ocean, green-brown continents and swirling white clouds, hazy and
desaturated; closer: a long space station truss spine with a habitat drum, blue solar panel
wings, docking module, cargo pods and a gun turret, dark violet alien growths clinging to it;
play plane: a white-grey interceptor firing blue bolts, a V of violet alien darts, a large
spider-like alien gunship firing rings of magenta bullets; foreground: tumbling hull debris,
slightly blurred; key light from top-left, shadows cast down-right onto the station, 1990s
pre-rendered CGI game background`

Layer contents: deep ×0.25 (Earth), ground ×1.0 (station + Vrell pods), air (play plane),
high-air ×1.75 (debris).

## parallax-r01-b

**Earth megacity at night**, layer breakdown on the sheet, 4 s seamless loop in the GIF.

Prompt: `top-down view straight down onto a futuristic megacity at night, 320x360 portrait game
screen, dense grid of dark skyscrapers seen from above with visible side walls in perspective,
thousands of warm and cool lit windows, rooftop details, helipads and red beacons, sodium-orange
street lights on a dark street grid, small hover cars with red trails flying low between towers,
alien spore turrets on some rooftops, a white-grey interceptor and violet alien fighters in the
air casting shadows onto the city, thin purple clouds lit orange from below drifting over,
1990s pre-rendered CGI, Blade Runner meets Raptor`

Layer contents: ground ×1.0 (streets + towers with true perspective parallax + rooftop turrets),
low-air ×1.25 (traffic), air (play plane), high-air ×1.75 (clouds, max ~40 % opacity).
