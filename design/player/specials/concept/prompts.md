# Specials — concept prompts

## specials-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py specials`
(`specials-r09-a.png` sheet and `specials-r09-a.gif`): Airstrike (two CDF bombers entering at
the player's x ± 64 px 0.6 s after the call, a bomb every 36 px bursting 0.25 s after release),
Smart Bomb (white flash 0.1 s at 80 % fading over 0.25 s, a ring from the ship covering the field
in 0.35 s and popping every bullet it passes, the ship blinking for 1 s) and Decoy Flares (four
flares in a 120° fan backwards, slowing to a stop in 0.6 s, homing shots retargeting and
detonating on them). The CDF bomber is a new SDF model in the generator.

**Prompt:** top-down effect frames for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical shoot'em up:
two heavy grey twin-engine bombers with blue wing bands and blue-white engine flames flying up
the screen and dropping a carpet of finned bombs that burst into a double line of orange
fireballs on the ground; a full-screen white flash and a bright white-cyan shockwave ring
expanding from the player ship, enemy bullets popping into small white sparkles as it passes;
four white-hot magnesium flares with sparkle spikes and grey smoke trails fanning out behind the
ship, enemy seeker missiles curving onto them and exploding; Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era look.

**Negative prompt:** magenta or yellow flares (reserved for enemy bullets), cartoon bombs,
photographic footage, text other than HUD captions, watermark.
