# Specials — concept prompts

## specials-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py specials`
(`specials-r09-a.png` sheet and `specials-r09-a.gif`): Airstrike (two CDF bombers entering at
the player's x ± 64 px 0.6 s after the call, a bomb every 36 px bursting 0.25 s after release),
Smart Bomb (white flash 0.1 s at 80 % fading over 0.25 s, a ring from the ship covering the field
in 0.35 s and popping every bullet it passes, the ship blinking for 1 s) and Decoy Flares (four
flares in a 120° fan backwards, slowing to a stop in 0.6 s, homing shots retargeting and
detonating on them). The CDF bomber is a new SDF model in the generator.

**Prompt:** top-down effect frames for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical shoot'em up:
two heavy grey twin-engine bombers with blue wing bands and blue-white engine flames flying up
the screen and dropping a carpet of finned bombs that burst into a double line of orange
fireballs on the ground; a full-screen white flash and a bright white-cyan shockwave ring
expanding from the player ship, enemy bullets popping into small white sparkles as it passes;
four white-hot magnesium flares with sparkle spikes and grey smoke trails fanning out behind the
ship, enemy seeker missiles curving onto them and exploding; Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era look.

**Negative prompt:** magenta or yellow flares (reserved for enemy bullets), cartoon bombs,
photographic footage, text other than HUD captions, watermark.

## airstrike-bomber-final-r17-a

Round 17, production art (M4 part D batch). Review sheet (`.png`) and loop (`.gif`) made from the final files in `assets/` by `tools/art/airstrike_bomber.py --review`; the frames themselves are rendered by `tools/art/airstrike_bomber.py` (see `tools/art/README.md`) from the chosen round-09 bomber model of `tools/concept/vfx_r09.py`, imported unchanged. Two sets:

- `airstrike-bomber_0..3` (56×64, facing up, 40 colours): the CDF bomber (grey hull, UTC blue wing bands, dark canopy), ray-marched at 8× at 26 px per model unit so the wings fit the 56 px width, with its two blue-white engine flames (2D light fields as in the concept, the soft edge stepped to four translucency levels) in four flicker lengths; the nose 3 px below the top edge, the hull's centre 4 px above the sprite's centre.
- `airstrike-bomber-shadow` (56×64): the hull's silhouette shrunk to 85 % about the sprite centre and blurred, near-black at up to 50 % opacity in four steps; the game draws it as is, offset on the ground layer (the concept used +34, +48 px).

The sheet shows the four frames and the shadow at 4× and all at 1×; the loop has three bombers crossing a Luna road at 600 px/s, their shadows on the ground. Not an image-generator prompt: the look is the chosen concept's, whose prompt stays valid; this is the brief for reviewing the production frames.

## smart-bomb-final-r26-a

Round 26, production art (M4 part H batch). Review sheet and loop made from `assets/` by `tools/art/smart_bomb.py --review`; the files are rendered by `tools/art/smart_bomb.py` (see `tools/art/README.md`). The Smart Bomb's flash and ring from the chosen round-09 specials sheet (`specials-r09-a`): `smart-bomb-ring` (72×8, additive: the ring's cross-section, a pale blue halo, a white core and a cyan band trailing inside, the sheet's own profile; the game draws it as a band of quads round the circle, so the ring stays crisp at every radius) and `smart-bomb-burst_0..11` (128×128, additive, 2 steps a frame: the blinding core where the bomb went off, a white-hot point, a pale blue bloom, a six-point star closing and white sparks). The white field flash stays the spec's fill (0.1 s at 80 %, fading over 0.25 s, toned down by the flash reduction). The sheet shows the profile, the burst and the whole effect over a dimmed Level 01 field every 4th step; the loop the same at 30 fps. The chosen sound (`special-smartbomb-r08-a`) is wired by the SFX pass. Not an image-generator prompt: the look is the chosen concept's, whose prompt stays valid; this is the brief for reviewing the production frames.
