# Wingmen – concept prompts

Prompts and briefs for polished versions of the round 02 wingman mockups. Generated by
[tools/concept/ships_r02.py](../../../../tools/concept/ships_r02.py) (the geometry is round 01
ship C, `ship_c_model` in `tools/concept/render/models.py`).

Shared direction: **Lt. Kenji "Rook" Tanaka's craft**, a Coalition Defence Force interceptor of
the same family as the player's AF-12 but with the blended-wing (manta) silhouette of ship
variant C: lifting body flowing into swept wings, sawtooth trailing edge, canards at the nose,
two canted dorsal fins, buried engines. Sprite size 40×40 at 960×540, slightly smaller than the
player's 48×48 so the player stays dominant. Seen straight from above, nose up, key light from
the top-left, late-90s pre-rendered CGI look in palette B. It must **never be mistaken for the
player at a glance**: the silhouette differs, and each scheme also differs in value or hue.

Common negative prompt: `perspective view, three-quarter view, side view, photo, painterly,
blurry, text, watermark, cartoon outline, anime, cel shading, forward-swept wings, needle
fuselage, blue-white hull with cyan stripes` (the last three are the player's look).

## rook-craft-r02-a

Scheme A, **"Ember"**: dark slate-violet hull (#4E5AA0 with #121632 shadows), orange wing panels
(#FF7A2A), yellow nose band and wingtips (#FFE04A), warm orange engine glow (#FF8830). Reads
darker and warmer than the player.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic render of a manta-shaped blended-wing sci-fi interceptor with
canards and a sawtooth trailing edge, dark slate violet metallic hull, bold orange wing panels,
yellow nose band and wingtips, dark blue canopy, warm orange engine glow, glossy specular,
key light from top-left, late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, plain dark background`

## rook-craft-r02-b

Scheme B, **"Jade"**: pale lime hull (#F0FFA0 with #003818 shadows), deep green panels (#007030),
yellow wingtips (#FFE04A), bright green engine glow (#40FF80). Same brightness as the player but a
clearly different hue.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic render of a manta-shaped blended-wing sci-fi interceptor with
canards and a sawtooth trailing edge, pale lime-green metallic hull, deep green leading-edge
panels, yellow wingtips, dark blue canopy, bright green engine glow, glossy specular, key light
from top-left, late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, plain dark background`


## rook-craft-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py rook`: Rook's craft (ship C
airframe, Ember scheme chosen in round 02) at 40×40 in the 5 banking frames (−28, −14, 0, +14,
+28°), the 320 px source render, a 1× strip beside the player's Stormhawk frames with a
greyscale check, and a 2× in-game escort view.

**Prompt:** five-frame banking sprite strip of a compact forward-swept sci-fi escort fighter,
dark slate hull, orange wing panels, yellow nose band and wingtips, dark blue canopy, warm
orange engine glow, rolled hard left, left, level, right and hard right, top-down view, key
light fixed at the top-left in every frame, glossy specular, late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game
sprite, 40×40 pixels, transparent background.

**Negative prompt:** blue or white hull (the player's colours), lime-green scheme, perspective
camera, motion blur, cartoon outlines, text, watermark.

## rook-ingame-capture-r28-a

## part-a-capture-final-r28-a

Concept round 28 (M5 part A), a capture of the game, not generated art (`.png` labelled sheet,
`.mp4` with the game's sound, 101 s), taken after part A's production sprites: the install script
of `:desktop:installDist` (atlases packed from the current `assets/`) started on a private Xvfb
display at 960×540 (settings: a 960×540 window at 0,0, `controls.auto-fire=true`), medium, every

game's mix to a file, nothing played aloud, aligned by the file's end time (checked on the eject:
the explosion's sound and its first frame within 0.05 s). The ship was moved by XTest key events
on that display (up once, then left/right sways). Level time t = video time − 2.43 to 2.5 s (the
first frame of the level; the Level 07 boss banner at t 99.95 as in round 25). Runs:

- **Level 06** (`--level 6 --escort rook:missiles:3,side=right --loadout
  front=hornet-launcher:3,left=swivel-gun:3,right=swivel-gun:3,rear=fan-blaster:3,utility=targeting-computer,utility2=salvage-scanner:2`,
  no `--invulnerable`): Rook in Wing and Wide, the Hornets, Swivels and Fan Blaster, his smoke,
  the rear-wave bark and the eject. **Scratch data:** this run read a copy of
  [data.yaml](../data.yaml) with `rook.armour: 5` placed first on the capture's classpath only
  (the real file is unchanged), since in seven earlier runs (Levels 01, 02, 03 and 07, two on hard,
  two with his dodging switched off in scratch data) he was never hit once. One 4-damage bullet at t 105.6 left him at 1 (smoke, the HUD number
  blinking red); the Coilwyrm's rear entry rammed him at t 136.2 (eject, `medium` explosion, the

- **Level 02** (`--level 2 --escort rook:missiles:3 --loadout …,rear=tail-gun:3,…`, scratch

- **Hangar**: `--bench 50 --start title` with a hand-placed save at Level 08 (`saves/slot-1.json`
  next to the scratch settings file, written by a test-made `SaveFormat.write`: Rook hired,
  Autocannon L2 fitted, Missiles L3 owned, his armour 34 / 80), Continue, then the slot tabs to

## rook-eject-capture-r28-b

Concept round 28 (M5 part A), a capture of the game, not generated art (`.png` labelled sheet),
taken after the eject fixes (the pod's path rule, drawn over the explosion after 0.1 s with its
beacon glow and smoke trail, the urgent eject bark). Method as
[part-a-capture-final-r28-a](#part-a-capture-final-r28-a): the install script of
`:desktop:installDist` on a private Xvfb display at 960×540 (a 960×540 window at 0,0,
`controls.auto-fire=true`), `--bench`, recorded with `ffmpeg -f x11grab -framerate 30`
(lossless), sound to a file through OpenAL Soft's `wave` driver (nothing played aloud), the ship
swayed by XTest key events on that display (up once, then left/right holds).
**Scratch data:** a copy of [data.yaml](../data.yaml) placed first on the run's classpath only (the
real file is unchanged) with `rook.armour: 2`, `rook.hitbox: [40, 40]` and `dodge.reacts: 0.0`, so
that one bullet ejects him: in four Level 06 runs with armour 2 (medium, then dodging off, then hard
with the sways, then the 40 px hitbox on medium with the sways; 100–150 s) he was never hit. The run: `--level 7 --escort rook:missiles:3,side=right
--loadout front=hornet-launcher:3,left=swivel-gun:3,right=swivel-gun:3,rear=fan-blaster:3,utility=targeting-computer,utility2=salvage-scanner:2
--bench 140`, medium; a bullet during the Brood Carrier fight ejected him at about video 137.1 s.

The sheet (frames at 10 fps, assembled with PIL): the eject at 3× zoom in six frames 0.1 s apart
(the flash; the pod over the explosion; its red beacon glow; the pod tumbling off with the grey
smoke puffs behind it), and the full frame 0.7 s after the eject: the pod heading for the right
edge, away from the player's ship on the left, `EJECTED` in the HUD's escort box and the eject bark
"I'm out, I'm out! She's all yours, Lancer!" opening on the radio at once.

Findings: the pod clears the explosion's edge within 0.1–0.2 s and reads by its blinking red glow
and its trail; the 16×16 pod itself is small at 1× but identifiable, so it was not re-rendered
larger (`tools/art/rook.py` unchanged). The pod took the nearer edge with the ship on the other side, so the in-the-way branch of the path
rule did not occur in this run (it is covered by `WingmanPodTest`). The bark
played at once, not held back by the spacing.
