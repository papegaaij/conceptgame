## pickups-r09-a

Round 09. Mockup generated by [tools/concept/vfx_r09.py](../../../tools/concept/vfx_r09.py) `python3 tools/concept/vfx_r09.py pickups`
(`pickups-r09-a.png` sheet and `pickups-r09-a.gif`): salvage S / M / L (credit chips, a chip
stack, a cargo crate), shield cell, armour patch, overdrive, special charge and data core as
small pre-rendered models (18–24 px) with an 8-frame turn (flat ones rock ±55° so they are never
seen edge-on) and a pulsing white outline and halo; a test next to enemy bullets in colour and
greyscale; the GIF shows drops drifting down among enemy bullets and being collected.

**Prompt:** sprite sheet of collectible power-ups for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical
shoot'em up, top-down view, 18 to 24 pixels, transparent background: silver hexagonal credit
chips with a glowing cyan inlay ring, a stack of three chips, a small armoured cargo crate with
straps and a cyan stripe, a glass capsule of blue shield energy between silver end caps, a
riveted steel plate with a raised green repair cross, two stacked chrome chevrons with violet
glowing cores, a silver ring around an amber four-point star, a pale glowing crystal held by a
dark metal clamp; each object glossy and lit from the top-left, with a thin pulsing white
outline and soft white halo; Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era render look.

**Negative prompt:** flat round glowing orbs (they would read as bullets), magenta, orange or
yellow as a main colour (reserved for enemy bullets), cartoon icons, letters on the pickups,
text, watermark.
