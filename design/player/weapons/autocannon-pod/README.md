# Autocannon Pod

## Summary

Cheap wing pod with a light forward cannon. Adds steady forward damage; most players fit a pair.

### Behaviour

- Per pod: fires from the pod's muzzle with a 2° convergence towards the ship's centre line (the two streams meet about 460 px ahead).
- The pod is drawn on the ship sprite at its wing mount ([ship](../../ship/README.md)).
