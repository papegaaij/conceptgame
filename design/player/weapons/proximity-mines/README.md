# Proximity Mines

## Summary

Drops mines behind the ship that detonate when an enemy comes close. Area denial against pursuers.

### Behaviour

- Mines drop from the rear muzzle with a 60 px/s backward drift that decays over 0.5 s, then hold their screen position on the `air` layer.
- A mine arms after 0.4 s and detonates when a hittable enemy on `air` or `low-air` comes within 40 px; the blast hits everything it may hit within the blast radius, once.
- Unexploded mines fizzle after 4 s. At the *max live* count no new mine drops until one is gone.
- Single-target DPS assumes every mine detonates on the target; mines dropped where no enemy passes are wasted.
