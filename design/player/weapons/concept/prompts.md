# Player weapons — concept prompts

## projectiles-r08-a

Round 08. Mockup generated by `tools/concept/vfx_r08.py projectiles`
(`projectiles-r08-a.png` sheet and `projectiles-r08-a.gif` loop of every family firing in
sequence from the Stormhawk at L3, Ion Beam at L5).

Families: pulse (Pulse Cannon), vulcan (Scatter Vulcan), ballistic (Autocannon Pod, Swivel
Gun), laser (Lance Laser), beam (Ion Beam), missile (Hornet Launcher), micro-missile
(Micro-missile Pod), mortar (Hammer Mortar, ground only), bomb (Bomb Rack, ground only),
torpedo (Torpedo Pod), mine (Proximity Mines), rear (Tail Gun, Fan Blaster), side (Side
Splitter). Each with muzzle flash (3 frames), impact (4 frames) and L1/L3/L5 patterns.

**Prompt:** sprite sheet of player projectiles for a late-1990s pre-rendered CGI vertical
shoot'em up, top-down view, 960x540 game scale, transparent background: glowing cyan-and-white
energy bolts with soft bloom, thin white tracer slugs with pale blue tails, long piercing cyan
laser lances, a continuous rippling ion beam, small white-and-blue guided missiles with blue-
white exhaust plumes and grey smoke trails, finned grey bombs, plasma mortar shells with a
blue glowing band, slim grey torpedoes with bubble trails, squat proximity mines with a
blinking blue sensor light; star-shaped muzzle flashes, white spark impacts and small
fireballs; chrome-and-neon palette (blue, cyan, white only for player shots), crisp
silhouettes, Raptor / Tyrian 2000 era render look.

**Negative prompt:** magenta, pink, yellow or orange shots (reserved for enemy bullets), flat
vector art, cartoon outlines, photographic realism, motion blur smears, side view,
perspective camera, text, watermark.
