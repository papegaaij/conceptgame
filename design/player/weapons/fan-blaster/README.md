# Fan Blaster

## Summary

Rear spread gun: a fan of shots angled back. Covers the whole lower screen against rear swarms.

### Behaviour

- A fan centred on straight back from the rear muzzle; shots fade at 390 px (0.6 s).
- From L4 the fan has a dense core (0 / ±10°) so it still hurts a single pursuer.

## Implementation

The Fan Blaster has flown since M4 part A with borrowed effects. M5 part A gives it its own effects (from a production generator) and its family's sound 10 % lower as a rear gun, reviewed in concept round 28, and checks the rest against this document.

- 2026-10-06: M5 part A (user decision D1 = a): built in part A with the other Act 2 weapons that need no water (Tail Gun, Fan Blaster, Hornet Launcher, Swivel Gun, Proximity Mines); the effects go straight to production (the families were chosen in round 08) and are reviewed in concept round 28. The Torpedo Pod and the `sub` layer move to part E (Level 11).
