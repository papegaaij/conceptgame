# Swivel Gun

## Summary

Wing pod with a turret that auto-aims at the nearest enemy in any direction, favouring the flanks and rear.

### Behaviour

- Per pod: the turret tracks the nearest hittable enemy within 300 px in any direction (slew 360°/s) and fires straight bullets at 700 px/s.
- Preference: an enemy outside the forward 60° cone wins when it is within 20 % of the nearest enemy's distance — the pod is meant for the flanks and rear.
- With no target in range it faces forward and does not fire.
