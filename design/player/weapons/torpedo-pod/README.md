# Torpedo Pod

## Summary

Wing pod that launches torpedoes into the water. Hits submerged and naval targets; optional in Act 2, essential in Act 4.

### Behaviour

- Per pod, **above water**: torpedoes drop into the water and run forward on the `sub` layer, accelerating from 300 to 420 px/s over 0.5 s, range 500 px, steering up to 60°/s towards the nearest `sub` or naval surface target in a 60° cone ahead.
- Over land or in space there is no water: the pod is idle and its HUD slot reads "NO WATER".
- **Under water** (Act 4) the torpedoes travel on the play plane, range +50 % (750 px), and hit every under-water target at full damage ([Europa rules](../../../world/europa/README.md#under-water-rules)).
