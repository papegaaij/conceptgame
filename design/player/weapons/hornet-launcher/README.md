# Hornet Launcher

## Summary

Front-mounted homing missile launcher. Finds targets on every hittable layer, including `high-air`.

### Behaviour

- Missiles launch from alternating ports at ±10 px with the listed launch angles, accelerating from 450 to 650 px/s over 0.3 s.
- At launch each missile picks the nearest hittable enemy (any layer it may hit, incl. `high-air`) within 420 px in a 120° cone ahead; it re-acquires if its target dies, otherwise flies straight. Lifetime 1.6 s.
- Turn rate as listed; the [targeting computer](../../systems/README.md) adds 20 %.
- Missiles cannot be shot down. Smoke trail per the round-08 VFX.
