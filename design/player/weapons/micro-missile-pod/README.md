# Micro-missile Pod

## Summary

Wing pod firing small homing missiles that curve out and in. The first homing weapon (L06).

### Behaviour

- Per pod: missiles launch outward at 30° (left pod to the left, right pod to the right), then home.
- Each missile picks the nearest hittable enemy (incl. `high-air`) within 350 px in a 140° cone ahead; re-acquires on target loss; lifetime 1.2 s; speed 500 px/s. The data's `range` is that seek
  radius, `lifetime` and `cone` the other two numbers. Missiles seek enemies only, never loot
  containers or a secret's beacon, but like every shot they hit a ground object they fly over.
- Turn rate as listed (+20 % with the targeting computer).
