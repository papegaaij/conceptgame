# Hammer Mortar

## Summary

Lobs shells that burst on the ground ahead. Big area damage against ground targets, including hardened ones; useless against flyers.

### Behaviour

- Shells are lobbed on a 0.6 s arc (sprite scale 1.0 → 1.4 → 1.0) and land 200 px ahead of the muzzle; multiple shells land at the listed side offsets.
- Auto-aim: a shell snaps to the nearest ground target whose centre is within 48 px of its landing point.
- The burst damages every ground unit within the blast radius once (hardened included; the ×2 anti-ground bonus is already in the damage). It never hits anything flying.
- A faint landing reticle shows where the next shell lands.
