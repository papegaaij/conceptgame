# Side Splitter

## Summary

Rear-slot gun that fires sideways, left and right at once. Covers enemies that hold at the screen edges.

### Behaviour

- Fires from both wing roots at 90° left and 90° right at the same time; the table lists one side.
- Range 360 px (0.45 s), enough to reach either edge from the centre of the 480 px play field.
- Takes the rear slot: choosing it means flying without a rear gun.
