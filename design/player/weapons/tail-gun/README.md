# Tail Gun

## Summary

The basic rear gun: straight bolts backwards for enemies that come from behind.

### Behaviour

- Bolts leave the rear muzzle (24, 45) straight down and fly until they leave the screen.
- Plays the `pulse` family about 10 % lower than the front gun.

## Implementation

The Tail Gun has flown since M4 part A with borrowed effects. M5 part A gives it its own effects (from a production generator) and its family's sound 10 % lower as a rear gun, reviewed in concept round 28, and checks the rest against this document.
