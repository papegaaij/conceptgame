# Player ship – concept prompts

Prompts and briefs for polished versions of the round 01 player ship mockups. The mockups are
generated by [tools/concept/player_ship.py](../../../../tools/concept/player_ship.py) (SDF
ray-marched models in `tools/concept/render/models.py`); rerun it to regenerate.

Shared direction for all variants: the **AF-12 Stormhawk**, a Coalition Defence Force (UTC)
interceptor, year 2185. Clean military grey/white hull with blue (UTC) and orange accents,
dark tinted canopy, glowing blue-white engines. Seen **straight from above**, nose pointing up,
lit by a key light from the **top-left**. It must read as a crisp late-90s pre-rendered CGI
sprite (Raptor: Call of the Shadows, Tyrian 2000): chunky readable silhouette, metallic
specular highlights, subtle panel lines, no painterly texture.

Common negative prompt: `perspective view, three-quarter view, side view, photo, painterly,
brush strokes, blurry, motion blur, text, watermark, logo, background clutter, cartoon outline,
anime, cel shading, lens flare, modern PBR realism, rust, damage`

## player-ship-r01-a

Variant A, **forward-swept wing**.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic render of a sleek sci-fi jet interceptor, forward-swept wings,
small canards near the nose, long needle fuselage, twin engines in dark nacelles at the rear,
twin vertical tail fins, wingtip gun rails, white-grey metallic hull with fine panel lines, blue
wingtip and spine stripes, orange nose band, dark blue glass canopy, glowing blue engine nozzles,
key light from top-left, 1990s pre-rendered 3D Studio / Softimage CGI look, glossy specular
highlights, centred on plain dark background, game sprite sheet reference`

Artist notes: aggressive, fast; the forward-swept wing is the recognisable feature. Must stay
readable at 32x32 px.

## player-ship-r01-b

Variant B, **twin-boom**.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic render of a sci-fi twin-boom fighter, central cockpit pod, two
long engine booms, straight tapered main wing, horizontal tailplane joining the booms, small
vertical fins, gun barrels at the front of each boom, white-grey metallic hull, blue wingtips
and tailplane, orange bands on the booms, dark tinted canopy, glowing blue-white engines, key
light from top-left, late 1990s pre-rendered CGI game sprite, glossy metal, plain dark background`

Artist notes: the workhorse look – wide, stable, iconic silhouette (P-38 lineage). The widest
of the three, which also makes hit-box expectations clear.

## player-ship-r01-c

Variant C, **blended wing with canards**.

Prompt: `top-down orthographic render of a futuristic blended-wing-body interceptor, manta-ray
shaped lifting body flowing into swept wings, sawtooth trailing edge, small canards at the nose,
two canted dorsal fins, engines buried in the body, white-grey metallic hull, blue leading-edge
stripes, orange wingtips, dark blue glass canopy, glowing engine slits, key light from top-left,
1990s pre-rendered CGI sprite style, glossy specular, plain dark background`

Artist notes: the most advanced, "sci-fi" option. Watch readability of the thin fins at sprite
size; the silhouette relies on the arrowhead outline.
