# Title and logo – concept prompts

Prompts and briefs for polished versions of the round 01 working-title / logo candidates. The
mockups are generated by [tools/concept/logos.py](../../../../tools/concept/logos.py).

Shared direction: a late-90s game logo for the main menu – chrome or metallic lettering with a
bevel lit from the top-left, strong outline, glow, over a starfield with a subtle nebula. It
must also work small (box art, window title) and in one colour.

Common negative prompt: `flat vector logo, minimalist, thin font, handwritten, grunge, 3D
perspective tilt, photo, watermark, misspelled text, extra letters, cluttered background`

## logo-r01-a

**STORMHAWK** – subtitle "THE VRELL WAR". Named after the player's ship, like *Raptor*.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "STORMHAWK", heavy italic sans-serif letters, classic
sky-and-ground chrome gradient (blue sky, white horizon, dark brown line, orange ground),
bevelled edges, thin orange outline and dark outer stroke, orange glow, lens flare glint on
the top edge, subtitle "THE VRELL WAR" in spaced white capitals below, starfield with purple and
blue nebula background, 3D Studio era chrome text`

## logo-r01-b

**AEGIS WING** – subtitle "DEFENDERS OF SOL – 2185". Named after the player's unit; stresses
defence.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "AEGIS WING", wide spaced bold capitals in polished gold
with a bright horizon line, bevelled, deep blue outline with blue glow, silver stylised eagle
wings emblem above the text, subtitle "DEFENDERS OF SOL - 2185" in spaced white capitals, deep
blue starfield background, military insignia feel, pre-rendered CGI metal`

## logo-r01-c

**LAST LINE 2185** – the stakes plus the year, very 90s.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "LAST LINE" in condensed bold brushed steel letters with
scratches, dark red outline, below it "2185" in glowing red LED digits with scanlines and red
bloom, dark red and navy nebula starfield background, gritty military sci-fi, pre-rendered CGI`

## logo-r01-d

**TERRAN VANGUARD** – the faction's front line.
