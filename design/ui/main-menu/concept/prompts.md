# Title and logo – concept prompts

Prompts and briefs for polished versions of the round 01 working-title / logo candidates. The
mockups are generated by [tools/concept/logos.py](../../../../tools/concept/logos.py).

Shared direction: a late-90s game logo for the main menu – chrome or metallic lettering with a
bevel lit from the top-left, strong outline, glow, over a starfield with a subtle nebula. It
must also work small (box art, window title) and in one colour.

Common negative prompt: `flat vector logo, minimalist, thin font, handwritten, grunge, 3D
perspective tilt, photo, watermark, misspelled text, extra letters, cluttered background`

## logo-r01-a

**STORMHAWK** – subtitle "THE VRELL WAR". Named after the player's ship, like *Raptor*.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "STORMHAWK", heavy italic sans-serif letters, classic
sky-and-ground chrome gradient (blue sky, white horizon, dark brown line, orange ground),
bevelled edges, thin orange outline and dark outer stroke, orange glow, lens flare glint on
the top edge, subtitle "THE VRELL WAR" in spaced white capitals below, starfield with purple and
blue nebula background, 3D Studio era chrome text`

## logo-r01-b

**AEGIS WING** – subtitle "DEFENDERS OF SOL – 2185". Named after the player's unit; stresses
defence.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "AEGIS WING", wide spaced bold capitals in polished gold
with a bright horizon line, bevelled, deep blue outline with blue glow, silver stylised eagle
wings emblem above the text, subtitle "DEFENDERS OF SOL - 2185" in spaced white capitals, deep
blue starfield background, military insignia feel, pre-rendered CGI metal`

## logo-r01-c

**LAST LINE 2185** – the stakes plus the year, very 90s.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "LAST LINE" in condensed bold brushed steel letters with
scratches, dark red outline, below it "2185" in glowing red LED digits with scanlines and red
bloom, dark red and navy nebula starfield background, gritty military sci-fi, pre-rendered CGI`

## logo-r01-d

**TERRAN VANGUARD** – the faction's front line.

Prompt: `1990s video game title logo "TERRAN VANGUARD" on two lines, bold capitals in
blue-tinted chrome with a sharp horizon reflection, deep 3D extrusion towards the bottom right
in dark navy, white inner stroke, cold blue glow, star glint lens flare at the top-left, blue
and violet nebula starfield background, late 90s CD-ROM game box art typography`

# Round 06 – main menu, difficulty select, load game

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/ui_r06.py](../../../../tools/concept/ui_r06.py)
(`python3 tools/concept/ui_r06.py menu difficulty load`). Each sheet shows the full 960×540
screen at 1× plus a 2× detail crop.

Shared direction: the same game as HUD A – brushed blue-violet metal plates with bevels and
rivets, recessed dark LCD wells, green/cyan/amber pixel text in a chunky 5×7 bitmap font,
palette B "90s Neon CGI", logo D. Late-90s PC game front end (pre-rendered title scene, 1997–99
CD-ROM shoot'em up). Selected items glow amber.

Common negative prompt: `modern flat UI, mobile game UI, rounded material design cards,
glassmorphism blur, thin fonts, anti-aliased modern typography, photographic background,
watermark, misspelled text, cluttered layout`

## main-menu-r06-a

Menu over a pre-rendered hero scene. Prompt: `late 1990s video game main menu screen 16:9,
pre-rendered CGI title scene: the curved limb of planet Earth with a glowing blue atmosphere
along the bottom, a bright sun with lens flare top left, a sleek white and blue jet-like
space interceptor with cyan engine trails climbing towards the top right, a swarm of small
purple biomechanical alien fighters with teal glows emerging from a dark nebula top right,
chrome blue extruded logo "TERRAN VANGUARD" at the top, a translucent dark menu panel with
thin metal trim on the right listing CONTINUE, NEW GAME, LOAD GAME, OPTIONS, CREDITS, QUIT in
white pixel capitals, NEW GAME highlighted with an amber gradient bar and three small
difficulty buttons EASY MEDIUM HARD, 3D Studio Max 1998 render look`

## main-menu-r06-b

Metallic console. Prompt: `late 1990s video game main menu as a full-screen metal control
console, brushed blue-violet steel panels with bevels, rivets and a yellow-black hazard strip
along the bottom, a recessed viewscreen at the top showing the chrome logo "TERRAN VANGUARD"
over a starfield with scanlines, a column of recessed dark LCD buttons in the centre with
green pixel text, the selected NEW GAME button lit amber with an amber side bar and three
inline difficulty buttons, left a small blueprint viewport with a top-down jet interceptor
sprite, right a "LAST SAVE" thumbnail of a red Mars canyon and a green text news ticker,
pre-rendered 1998 game UI`
