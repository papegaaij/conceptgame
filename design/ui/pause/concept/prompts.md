# Pause – concept prompts

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/ui_r08.py](../../../../tools/concept/ui_r08.py) (`python3 tools/concept/ui_r08.py pause`). Each sheet shows the full 960×540 screen at 1× plus a 2× detail crop.

Shared direction (glass style, the out-of-game screens since round 06): translucent dark navy glass panels with a thin brushed-metal trim and small corner tabs over a dimmed pre-rendered late-90s CGI scene; white and cyan pixel text in a chunky bitmap font, amber for selection and money, an amber gradient selection bar with a triangular cursor, small rectangular chips; palette B (90s neon CGI). Portraits are the style-B comm-screen portraits with retained colour (round 04).

Common negative prompt: `modern flat UI, mobile game, rounded cards, thin modern fonts, bevelled riveted metal panels (reserved for the in-game HUD), photographic background, watermark, misspelled text, cluttered or illegible layout`

## pause-r08-a

Pause menu over the dimmed in-game frame (HUD r08, mission 11).

Prompt: `late 1990s shoot'em up pause menu, 16:9, the in-game screen with metal side panels dimmed and softly blurred behind, a centred translucent glass panel with an amber 'PAUSED' title, mission name and time, a vertical menu RESUME (amber selection bar), RESTART MISSION, OPTIONS, ABORT TO HANGAR, QUIT TO MAIN MENU, chunky pixel font`

Negative prompt: the common negative prompt above.

## pause-capture-final-r13-a

A capture of the game, not generated art: `desktop/build/install/terran-vanguard/bin/terran-vanguard --bench <s> --settings <file> --start level` on a private 960×540 Xvfb display (settings in a temporary directory, so the saves are too: `display.mode=window`, a 960×540 window at 0,0, `audio.master=0`), the keys sent into the game window with XTest and the screen grabbed with ImageMagick `import`: Escape after 9 s of Level 01.
