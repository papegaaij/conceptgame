# Shared UI kit – concept prompts

Mockup generated by [tools/concept/ui_r08.py](../../../tools/concept/ui_r08.py) (`python3 tools/concept/ui_r08.py kit`). The sheet collects the reusable widgets of both styles and the proposed production bitmap fonts (DejaVu Sans Mono Bold rasterised 1-bit into fixed 8×12, 10×20 and 20×30 cells); the screen mockups still use the 5×7 placeholder font. The game's fonts are this rasterisation, written as BMFont files into `assets/fonts/` by [tools/concept/ui_assets.py](../../../tools/concept/ui_assets.py).

Shared direction (glass style, the out-of-game screens since round 06): translucent dark navy glass panels with a thin brushed-metal trim and small corner tabs over a dimmed pre-rendered late-90s CGI scene; white and cyan pixel text in a chunky bitmap font, amber for selection and money, an amber gradient selection bar with a triangular cursor, small rectangular chips; palette B (90s neon CGI). Portraits are the style-B comm-screen portraits with retained colour (round 04).

Common negative prompt: `modern flat UI, mobile game, rounded cards, thin modern fonts, bevelled riveted metal panels (reserved for the in-game HUD), photographic background, watermark, misspelled text, cluttered or illegible layout`

## ui-kit-r08-a

Glass widgets (panel, header, menu items in states, chips, primary button, slider, toggle, tabs, list rows, confirm dialog), metal HUD widgets (plate, LCD well, segmented bars, pips, LCD colours, special icons, radio, escort, hazard strip) and font specimens.

Prompt: `late 1990s PC game UI style sheet, two halves: left translucent dark glass widgets with thin metal trim over a dim space scene (menu items normal / selected with an amber bar / disabled, small chips, an amber primary button, slider, toggle, tabs, list rows with diamond markers, a confirm dialog), right bevelled brushed blue-violet metal HUD widgets with recessed LCD wells and segmented red, cyan and magenta bars; below, bitmap font specimen sheets in three sizes with the full character set, crisp pixels`

Negative prompt: the common negative prompt above.
