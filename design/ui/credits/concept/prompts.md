# Credits – concept prompts

Mockups generated by [tools/concept/ui_r08.py](../../../../tools/concept/ui_r08.py) (`python3 tools/concept/ui_r08.py credits`). Each sheet shows the full 960×540 screen at 1× plus a 2× detail crop. The CC-BY attributions are read from [CREDITS.md](../../../../CREDITS.md).

Shared direction (glass style, the out-of-game screens since round 06): translucent dark navy glass panels with a thin brushed-metal trim and small corner tabs over a dimmed pre-rendered late-90s CGI scene; white and cyan pixel text in a chunky bitmap font, amber for selection and money, an amber gradient selection bar with a triangular cursor, small rectangular chips; palette B (90s neon CGI). Portraits are the style-B comm-screen portraits with retained colour (round 04).

Common negative prompt: `modern flat UI, mobile game, rounded cards, thin modern fonts, bevelled riveted metal panels (reserved for the in-game HUD), photographic background, watermark, misspelled text, cluttered or illegible layout`
